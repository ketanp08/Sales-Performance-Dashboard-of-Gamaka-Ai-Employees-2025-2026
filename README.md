# Gamaka AI – Sales Dashboard 2025–26 | Power BI Project

## 📌 Project Overview
Developed an interactive Sales Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze Gamaka AI's sales performance for the financial year 2025–26. The dashboard provides insights into sales, costs, profits, product categories, brand performance, and state-wise sales distribution.

## 🎯 Key Features
- **Sales Performance Analysis:** Analyzed overall sales, costs, and profit.
- **Category Analysis:** Compared product quantities across different categories.
- **Brand Performance:** Visualized brand-wise contribution to total sales.
- **Geographical Analysis:** Analyzed state-wise sales using an interactive map.
- **Supervisor Overview:** Displayed supervisor information in the dashboard.
- **KPI Cards:** Presented important business metrics in one place.
- **Interactive Visualizations:** Created charts and visuals for easy business analysis.

## 📊 Key Business Metrics
- Total Sales: 16M
- Total Cost: 12M
- Total Profit: 4M
- Total Quantity: 2K
- Total Transactions: 794

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Total sales reached approximately 16 million during 2025–26.
- The business generated approximately 4 million in profit.
- Monitor was the highest-selling category by quantity, with 304 units.
- CPU ranked second with 254 units.
- Intel contributed the highest brand-wise sales share at 20.45%.
- Dell contributed 15.25% of total brand sales.
- The dashboard highlights sales distribution across different states.
